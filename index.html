Every verification code, exactly where you need it

OTPanda is being built to collect your one-time codes from SMS, email and your authenticator, and to put them on every device you use. It will even hand a code to an automation you trust, so you never copy one by hand again.

One home for every code

OTPanda is in development. This is what it is being built to do.

SMS codes, captured

The Android app will spot one-time codes in incoming text messages and file them automatically, the moment they arrive.

Email codes, in real time

With Gmail or Outlook connected, OTPanda will lift codes out of new mail as it lands. No polling, no waiting.

Built-in authenticator

TOTP secrets will live in OTPanda too, generating authenticator codes next to everything it collects.

On every device

Your codes will be reachable from the web app, the Android app and a browser extension. Whatever is closest when you need them.

Scoped tokens for automations

You will be able to give a script or an AI assistant a scoped token that fetches one kind of code, and nothing more. Your inbox stays yours.

Share one entry, not your inbox

You will be able to share a single service's codes with someone you choose, for as long as you choose, and revoke it in one tap.

How it works

How OTPanda will work once it launches.

1

Connect your sources

Link SMS, Gmail or Outlook, and add TOTP secrets. You will choose exactly what OTPanda can see.

2

Codes land in one place

New codes will appear in your feed, matched to the service and account they belong to.

3

Use them anywhere

You will copy a code from any device, or let an automation you authorized fetch it for itself.

Honest about how your codes are handled

OTPanda is being built as a delivery service for your codes, not a zero-knowledge vault. By design it stores codes on its servers so your devices, and only the automations and people you authorize, can reach them. You will be able to revoke any source, token or share at any time.
