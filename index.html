One home.
Every kind of code.

Stop bouncing between inboxes, messages and devices. Otty brings the right code to you.

01 / TEXT MESSAGES

Your phone can stay put.

The Android app picks up codes from incoming SMS and sends them to your OTPanda feed. No phone hunt required.

02 / EMAIL

Skip the inbox detour.

Connect Gmail or Outlook. Codes arrive in real time as new mail lands, without waiting for a periodic inbox check.

03 / AUTHENTICATOR

Six digits. Zero juggling.

A built-in TOTP authenticator keeps rotating codes alongside your SMS and email codes.

04 / YOUR DEVICES

Right here beats over there.

Use your codes from the web, Android or your browser toolbar. One feed, wherever you're signing in.

05 / YOUR AUTOMATIONS

Let your assistant get it.

Give a script or AI assistant a scoped API token for the entries you choose. Access to a code, not your whole inbox.

06 / YOUR PEOPLE

Share the entry. Not everything.

Share codes for a specific entry with someone you choose. Keep other entries private, and revoke access when you're done.

Trust, without the fine print

A delivery service.
Not a zero-knowledge vault.

OTPanda is designed to process codes on its servers so it can deliver them to your devices, people and automations. That means the service can read your codes. You control which entries you share and which tokens you issue, and can revoke sources, tokens and shares.
